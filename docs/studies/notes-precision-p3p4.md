# P3, P4 and a card-heading refinement after round 5: frozen rules and the replay (2026-10-29)

The [notes after round 5](notes-precision-round5.md) measured offline two small deterministic changes besides P1 and P2, which [shipped first](notes-precision-p1p2.md): a 1.1.1 reading claim on a social network's icon whose alternative names the network is no finding (P3), and a 2.4.4 shared-text claim on a link whose text names the page it leads to is no finding (P4). The P1/P2 replay also left a known cost on a test fixture: P1's card heading counted a heading that says nothing ("Section 2") as context. These notes record what was built, the rules and thresholds frozen before the confirming replay, and that replay.

**What each number is.** The second to fourth studies (s2–s4, all halves) and the fifth study's dev half were read, and P3 and P4 were chosen on s2–s4: every number on them is **in-sample**. The fifth study's test half was held out from the design of P1 to P4 but its counts were looked at by the round-5 notes and the P1/P2 replay: it is **counted** here, never read (no page, message or alternative of it is printed or looked at). The refinement was chosen on a test fixture, not on a study. Anything about pages not yet collected is **projected**. The labels are the studies' two Claude judges, not people.

## 1. What was built

- **P3, a social network's icon named for the network** (`src/criteria/non-text-content.ts`, `namesNetworkOfLink`, `groundedImage`, and `grounded` on the 1.1.1 criterion). A reading claim (the alternative "describes something the image does not show", or a functional image's alternative "does not say where the link leads") on an image that is all its link holds, when the link leads to a social network's host and the alternative names that network, is the model's suggestion, under "Needs review", and is asked of no second pass.
- **P4, a shared text that names its destination** (`src/criteria/link-purpose.ts`, `namesOwnDestination`, in `groundedLink`). A 2.4.4 claim that other links share the text and lead elsewhere is no finding when the link's text is the main heading, or one part of the title, that Rampa read at this link's address.
- **The card-heading refinement** (`src/criteria/link-purpose.ts`, `genericCardHeading`, in `cardHeadingOf`). A heading on Rampa's own generic lists is no card heading, so it no longer completes a generic link text. The shop fixture (`test/fixtures/checkout.html`), whose link P1 had moved a level down, and the integration tests' expectations on it (`test/playwright.test.ts`, `test/playwright-runner.test.ts`) are back to their form before P1: "Click here" under the cart's "Section 2" is a finding again, and a unit test covers the case.
