# P1 and P2 after round 5: frozen rules and the replay (2026-10-29)

The [notes after round 5](notes-precision-round5.md) proposed, measured offline and ranked first two deterministic changes: a 2.4.4 `generic` claim is no finding when the link's card has a heading or when the link is a disclosure toggle (P1), and identical 1.1.1 reading claims on one page are confirmed together by the second pass (P2). These notes record what was built from them, the rules and thresholds frozen before the confirming replay, and that replay.

**What each number is.** The second to fourth studies (s2–s4, all halves) and the fifth study's dev half were read, and P1 and P2 were chosen on them: every number on them is **in-sample**. The fifth study's test half was held out from the design of P1 and P2, but the round-5 notes counted each rule's effect on it once: it is **read-once** data, **counted** here and never read (no page, message or alternative of it is printed or looked at). Anything about pages not yet collected is **projected**. The labels are the studies' two Claude judges, not people.

## 1. What was built

- **P1, a card's heading completes a generic link text (technique H80), and a disclosure toggle is not a link to judge** (`src/criteria/link-purpose.ts`, `cardHeadingOf`, `menuToggleOf`, `groundedLink`). From the prototype `study/notes-precision-round5/data/g1-variant.diff`, with two choices of this port: the card heading is read only for a generic link text (the one claim it completes), so every other link's prompt stays as it was; and the toggle fact is read only for a generic text too. The prompt gains one line, for those links only.
- **P2, identical reading claims on one page are confirmed together** (`src/core/confirm.ts`, `identicalClaimKey`, `confirmTogether`; `src/core/check.ts`). The second pass now asks every claim that reaches it before any is sorted into findings and suggestions, in the same order as before, and then decides groups of identical claims. A claim its group confirms carries `confirmation.identical` in JSON (and the schema), and the terminal and Markdown say "confirmed by a second pass on an identical claim on this page (1 of 7 confirmed on their own)".

## 2. Frozen rules and thresholds

Written down before the confirming replay of section 3; nothing below was changed after it.

| Rule | Value | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Card heading (H80) | the nearest heading before the link in document order (visible, with a name), when it and the link share an ancestor at most **2** levels above the link (1 is its parent) whose whole text (`subtreeText`) is at most **300** characters; the lowest shared ancestor decides; read only when the link text is generic (`genericLinkText`) | `link-purpose.ts`, `cardHeadingOf`, `CARD_DEPTH`, `CARD_TEXT` |
| Card heading in the prompt | one line, "Heading of the card the link sits in: …", after "Heading the link sits under", only on links that have a card heading; the system prompt and the criterion's version (3) are unchanged, so every other cached 2.4.4 answer is reused | `link-purpose.ts`, `prompt` |
| Disclosure toggle | a generic link text whose `href` is absent, empty, `#` or `javascript:`, and whose nearest enclosing list item has, after the child that holds the link, a child that is or holds a `list` (shown or hidden); verification only, not in the prompt | `link-purpose.ts`, `menuToggleOf` |
| `generic` grounded | a generic text with no sentence, paragraph, list item or cell, no header cells, no description, **no card heading and no toggle**; or a text that names only a file's kind with nothing around it (unchanged) | `link-purpose.ts`, `groundedLink` |
| Judged first under the cap | unchanged rule (`groundable`, which reads `groundedLink`): a generic link P1 takes the fact from is no longer judged first, so on a page with more than 50 links other links take its place | `link-purpose.ts`, `judgeFirst` |
| Identical claims (P2) | on one page (one `checkSnapshot`), the claims the second pass was asked about with the same criterion, the same subject (1.1.1: the alternative text) after NFKD, combining marks removed, spaces collapsed and trimmed, lower case, and the same verdict (every claim of the pass is a `fail`); a claim with an empty subject is in no group | `confirm.ts`, `identicalClaimKey` |
| Group rule ("C-any") | in a group of two or more, when the pass confirmed at least one claim on its own answers (every framing confirms, lowest probability ≥ 0.99, unchanged), every claim of the group whose own outcome is `denied` is confirmed; a claim with no cached answer offline, an error or a provider that cannot answer one word keeps its outcome; a group with no confirmed claim changes nothing | `confirm.ts`, `confirmTogether` |
| What a group-confirmed finding says | `confirmation: { framings, minProbability (its own), identical: { claims, confirmed } }` | `check.ts`, `schema/report.schema.json` |
| Burst group and counting | as [notes-precision-fixes](notes-precision-fixes.md) §2 (default threshold, matched by page, criterion and element, Wilson 95%, groups of 6 or more one unit, page bootstrap with seed 20261027); the fifth study's labels from its `data/findings.json`, every origin (main run, then the `--no-confirm` ablation, then the suggestion sample), as for the fourth | `study/notes-precision-p1p2/analyze.py`, `labels.py` |

**The replay** (`study/notes-precision-p1p2/replay.sh`): each recorded snapshot of s2–s4 (72 pages) and of the fifth study (24 pages, 12 dev and 12 test), the studies' flags (`--min-confidence low --time-limit 900 --concurrency 2`), Gemma 4 12B through Ollama, each page run in its own work directory, and a fresh copy of a judgment cache: for s2–s4, the precision-fixes replay's final caches (`rampa-study-recordings/notes-precision-fixes-2026-10-27/caches-after`); for the fifth study, its main run's cache. s2's 2.4.4 and 2.4.6 are carried from the second study's re-judged run as in the precision-fixes replay, with P1's facts applied to them from `data/prompts.json` (they take the fact from none of s2's links). **Before** is main at `0bada65`, offline. **After** is this branch's build at the P2 commit (`data/after-commit.txt`), online: what its prompts change is asked of the model and kept in the work copies. Then `compact.py` and `analyze.py main p1p2`.
